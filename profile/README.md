# PrintFoot Lab

**3D printed recovery slides**, designed and printed to order in Los Angeles.

Official site: **[printfootlab.com](https://printfootlab.com/?src=gh-pr)**

- **What we make:** slip-on recovery slides for after runs and long days on your feet, printed in one piece from elastomeric resin by DLP (resin) 3D printing — no glued layers or separate foam sole.
- **Why a lattice:** an open lattice instead of solid EVA foam lets air and water through, and lets cushioning be tuned zone by zone (heel, arch, forefoot).
- **Designs:** [honeycomb, Voronoi and foam-lattice hybrid slides](https://printfootlab.com/designs?src=gh-pr), from US$99.
- **Guides:** [how 3D printed slides are made](https://printfootlab.com/guides/how-3d-printed-slides-are-made?src=gh-pr), [3D printed vs EVA foam slides](https://printfootlab.com/guides/3d-printed-vs-eva-foam-slides?src=gh-pr), [are 3D printed shoes waterproof?](https://printfootlab.com/guides/are-3d-printed-shoes-waterproof?src=gh-pr)
